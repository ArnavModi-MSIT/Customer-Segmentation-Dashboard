🎯 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis, combined with an XGBoost churn-risk model. Customers are segmented into seven groups to support targeted retention and engagement strategies. All metrics are computed on the true customer identity (customer_unique_id), not the per-order key the raw dataset exposes by default — a distinction that materially changes every downstream number.

Customer Segments

  • Champions: Best customers, highest value
  • Loyal Customers: Consistent purchasers
  • Potential Loyalist: High conversion potential
  • Need Attention: Largest segment by size
  • At Risk: Elevated churn risk
  • New Customers: Recent, low frequency
  • Lost: Inactive for an extended period

Business Intelligence

Key Business Insights

📈

Revenue Concentration

Champions (7% of customers) generate 13.15% of revenue — a real but modest concentration, not the inflated 80/20-style split a naive analysis can produce when customer identity is computed incorrectly.

🎯

Segmentation Reality Check

"Need Attention" is the largest segment at ~40% of customers — a direct consequence of a 3.12% repeat-purchase rate in this dataset. Most customers are one-time buyers, which shapes every retention strategy built on top of this data.

🔮

Churn Risk Scoring

An XGBoost model scores churn risk (ROC-AUC 0.65) using payment, delivery, and review-score behavior. Given how few customers repeat-purchase, this is honestly reported as a modest-but-real signal rather than an inflated accuracy figure.

⭐

Service Quality & Risk

Low-risk customers rate their experience 1.23x higher than high-risk customers on average — review score is the strongest single signal in the churn model.

📊

Data-Driven Segmentation

RFM scoring on the correct customer identity provides a quantifiable, reproducible basis for customer valuation, replacing per-order artifacts with genuine customer-level metrics.

💰

Retention ROI

Recovering just 10% of high-risk revenue (R$1.03M at risk) would add roughly R$103K — modeled directly in the dashboard's retention scenarios.

Technical Approach

Methodology & Implementation

01

Ingestion

Raw Olist CSVs loaded into PostgreSQL, with product category nulls cleaned and English category names joined in.

02

EDA

Revenue, order, customer, and review metrics computed on order-level (not item-level) aggregates for correctness.

03

RFM Segmentation

Recency, Frequency, and Monetary scores computed on true customer identity, mapping customers to 7 segments.

04

Churn Feature Engineering

Payment, delivery, and review-based features aggregated per customer, avoiding merge fan-out across multi-item, multi-payment orders.

05

Churn Modeling

XGBoost classifier trained on customer-level features, evaluated by ROC-AUC rather than accuracy alone given class imbalance.

06

Visualization

All results written back to PostgreSQL and served through an interactive Power BI dashboard across 3 pages.
